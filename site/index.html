How membership works

Membership is free. Paying dues supports the chapter and puts you on the points system.

  1. Come to any event, free

    Every general meeting, workshop and social is open to all WSU students. You don't need to pay to be a general body member.

  2. Pay dues to earn points

    Dues are optional. Once you've paid, every event you attend earns points toward the semester leaderboard.

  3. Climb the leaderboard

    Top point earners get chapter opportunities, like possibly having their trip to the SASE National Convention paid for.
