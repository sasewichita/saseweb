Mentorship program

SASE Ignite

Ignite connects high school and college students with mentors who can help them grow academically and professionally.

Apply now

How it works

  1. Apply

    Fill out one short form as a mentor or a mentee. It takes about 5 minutes.

  2. Get matched

    Officers pair mentors and mentees by goals, major, availability and how you'd like to meet.

  3. Meet up

    You'll get each other's contact info and set your own schedule, in person or virtually.

Mentees

High school students and WSU students at any year. Your mentor can help with:

  • Choosing classes and studying
  • Picking a major or career path
  • Internships, co-ops and job search
  • Resume, LinkedIn and interviews
  • Research opportunities
  • Getting involved on campus
  • Applying to college and the move from high school
  • Networking and professional development
  • Personal projects and technical skills

Mentors

WSU upperclassmen, graduate students, alumni and industry professionals.

Share what you've learned from your classes, internships, research or projects. You choose how many mentees to take on, from one to three or more, and whether you'd like to mentor high school students, college students, or both.
