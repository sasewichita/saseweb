What Hack the Wu is

Teams spend a weekend building something new, from an app to a hardware project, then show it off at the end. This is our second year running it.

  1. Beginner friendly

    Never been to a hackathon or written much code? You're exactly who we built this for.

  2. Open to all students

    Undergrad and grad students are both welcome to join.

  3. Every major welcome

    Design, business, science and engineering students all bring something a team needs.

How the weekend goes

  1. 01

    Team up

    Teams need designers, planners and presenters as much as programmers.

  2. 02

    Build something new

    Spend the weekend on anything from an app to a hardware project.

  3. 03

    Show it off

    At the end, every team shows what they built.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Hack the Wu is beginner friendly, and teams need designers, planners and presenters as much as programmers.

Who can attend?

Any undergrad or grad student, from any major.

When is it?

March 26–28 at Wichita State University. Join our Discord for the full schedule and venue announcements.

Who runs it?

The Society of Asian Scientists and Engineers (SASE) chapter at Wichita State.
