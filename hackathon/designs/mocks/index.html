Nine feels, one eye

Every one of these grows from the Panorama goat eye. Each is a mock: a hero, the scroll into the page, one content screen, and its navigation and cursor. Open them on a laptop and on a phone, then pick the ones to build in full.

  1. 1 Panorama The baseline, as built

    A three-ink print: yellow, blue and black on bone. Ruled lines, a bar cursor, the pupil opens into the page.
  2. 2 Torch Night watch

    The eye as a dark relief, lit only by a flame at the pointer. Text brightens as the light comes near.
  3. 3 Lino Cut by hand

    A one-colour block print. Heavy black, gouged lines that wobble and break, one flat block of yellow.
  4. 4 Halftone Under the loupe

    The eye in coarse two-colour dot screens. The dots swell under the pointer and break apart as you scroll.
  5. 5 Cyanotype Plate, fig. 1

    A blueprint. Thin white drafting lines on Prussian blue, a crosshair cursor with a live bearing, a title block.
  6. 6 Eyes They all look at you

    A wall of small eyes that all follow the pointer and blink on their own. The middle one holds the title.
  7. 7 Slot You are the goat

    A black screen with one wide slot. Through it, a level print landscape and a title too big to see at once.
  8. 8 Blacklight The second print

    A quiet outline print. A light at the pointer reveals the colour, and notes that are hidden without it.
  9. 9 Type eye Read the eye

    The eye built only from words. The letters stretch toward the pointer, and the lids are lines of text.

Mocks only. Nothing here is the final site, and the copy is last year's preview copy.
