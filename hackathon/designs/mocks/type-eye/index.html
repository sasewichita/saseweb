What Hack the Wu is

Teams spend a weekend building something new, from an app to a hardware project, then show it off at the end. This is our second year running it.

  • Beginner friendly Never been to a hackathon or written much code? You're exactly who we built this for.
  • Open to all students Undergrad and grad students are both welcome to join.
  • Every major welcome Design, business, science and engineering students all bring something a team needs.
